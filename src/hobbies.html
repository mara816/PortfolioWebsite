My Interests

Technology

I have a great passion for new technology and constantly follow technological developments. I am particularly interested in how technology can be used to solve problems and improve our everyday lives.

I enjoy experimenting with new gadgets, software, and programming languages to expand my understanding and skills in the technology field.

Running

Running is one of my preferred ways to stay active and clear my mind. I run regularly and enjoy both the physical and mental benefits it provides.

I have participated in several fun runs and find great joy in setting new goals and challenging myself. Running also gives me time to think creatively and find solutions to complex problems.

Skiing

Skiing is a passion I have pursued for many years. I love the feeling of freedom and speed when skiing, and the amazing nature in the mountains.

Every winter I try to go on at least one ski trip where I can enjoy the combination of physical activity, fresh air, and beautiful surroundings. It gives me energy and inspiration for my work.

Reading

I am an avid reader and find great joy in immersing myself in books about technology, innovation, and personal development. Reading is a way for me to continue my learning and expand my horizons.

I also read fiction to stimulate my creativity and imagination, which I find valuable in my work with problem-solving and innovation.

Work-Life Balance

I believe in the importance of a healthy work-life balance. My interests help me maintain this balance and provide me with energy and inspiration for my professional work.

By pursuing my interests, I stay physically active, mentally sharp, and creatively inspired. This is reflected in my approach to work, where I bring energy, creativity, and a positive attitude to all projects.
